How it works

Open a video. See everything.

NODUS YT Radar runs silently in the background. No setup. Just install and open any YouTube video.

1

Install the extension

Add NODUS YT Radar to Chrome or Firefox. Free, lightweight, no login required.

2

Open any YouTube video

Click the extension icon. Instantly see its rank in your country's Top 50.

3

Analyze & discover

Check Heat, Velocity, Momentum, and view history. Discover what's really trending.

Features

Everything you need to understand a video's performance

🏆

Real-Time Ranking

See if the video is in your country's Top 50 trending. Exact position or estimated rank with views needed to break in.

🔥

Heat Score

A 0–100 viral score combining growth speed, engagement rate, view count vs Top 50, and recency. Know if it's rising or peaking.

⚡

Velocity & Momentum

How fast is the video growing right now? Compare its hourly view rate to the average Top 50 video in your region.

📈

View History

Sparkline chart showing view count evolution over time. Delta pills for +1h, +6h, +24h growth. Detects acceleration.

🌍

25+ Regions

Auto-detects your country from the YouTube page. Switch to any of 25+ regions to compare rankings globally.

🎯

Category Filter

Filter Top 50 by category — Music, Gaming, News, Sports, and more. Auto-detected from the video you're watching.
