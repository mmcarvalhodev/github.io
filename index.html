Frequently asked questions

Questions & answers

The most common questions about NODUS — answered in seconds.

Is NODUS free?

Yes, the core features are free forever. Save, organize, search and export your captures without paying anything. The PRO plan unlocks additional queues, unlimited Chains and advanced export.

How do I save a ChatGPT conversation?

Install NODUS and a floating button appears on all supported platforms. Click Save to capture the response into your local panel — no copy and paste, no account required.

Does it work with Claude, Gemini and other AIs?

NODUS supports 7 platforms: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok and DeepSeek. The same button appears on all of them — everything goes into a single unified panel.

Is my data private?

Nothing leaves your device. NODUS stores everything in your browser's local storage — no own server, no account, no cloud sync.

Which browsers are supported?

NODUS works on Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Brave. Any Chromium-based browser works automatically. Install once, works across all AI platforms.

Can I export my captures?

Yes. NODUS exports in HTML, Markdown and DOC (Word). With Chains, export multiple captures together as a structured document.

How do Chains work?

Chains connect captures from different conversations — from different platforms, on different dates — into a logical sequence. A thread of reasoning that spans your entire AI history.

If I uninstall, do I lose everything?

Before uninstalling, use the full export to back up all your captures as JSON. Data lives in local storage — uninstalling clears that storage.
