Getting Started

6 questions

NODUS is a browser extension that lets you save, organize and reuse AI responses with one click. It works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Copilot and DeepSeek. Everything is stored locally on your device — no account, no server, no mandatory subscription.

Installation takes less than 30 seconds:

  • Chrome / Edge / Brave: visit the Chrome Web Store and click "Add to Chrome"
  • Firefox: visit Mozilla Add-ons and click "Add to Firefox"

After installing, open any supported AI platform — NODUS buttons appear automatically.

No. NODUS works without registration, no email, no password. Install and start using immediately. Your data stays in the browser's local storage.

NODUS works in:

  • Chrome (version 88+)
  • Firefox (version 109+)
  • Edge (Chromium-based)
  • Brave and other Chromium browsers

When you open any supported platform, NODUS injects floating buttons next to AI responses. Click Save to capture the question and response instantly. Click Quick to save without opening the settings panel. Click Dash to open your captures dashboard.

NODUS currently supports 7 platforms:

  • ChatGPT (chat.openai.com)
  • Claude (claude.ai)
  • Gemini (gemini.google.com)
  • Perplexity (perplexity.ai)
  • Grok (grok.com / x.com)
  • Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com)
  • DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com)

Export & Download

7 questions

Open your NODUS dashboard, select the desired capture and click Export → DOCX. The Word file is generated directly in your browser and saved to your computer. You can chain multiple captures with Chains and export everything together as a structured document.

NODUS exports to HTML, which you can print as a PDF directly from the browser (Ctrl+P → Save as PDF). For direct PDF export, we recommend exporting as HTML and using the browser's print function — the result is well formatted.

NODUS supports export in:

  • TXT — plain text, compatible with any editor
  • HTML — formatted, printable as PDF
  • DOCX — compatible with Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice

Chained captures (Chains) can be exported together as a single document.

In the NODUS dashboard, go to Settings → Export all. This generates a file with all your captures in JSON format (for reimporting into NODUS), or you can export each queue individually as DOCX or HTML.

In the card view in the dashboard, click Copy. The content is copied with Markdown formatting preserved — headings, lists, code — ready to paste into any editor that supports Markdown.

Yes. In Settings → Export data, you download a JSON file with all your captures. This file can be reimported into NODUS on another computer or after reinstalling the extension.

Use Chains to connect related captures from different sessions (even from different platforms). Then export the entire chain as DOCX — the result is a structured document with all questions and responses chained in logical order.

Organize

7 questions

When saving a capture, you can add up to 4 tags to categorize the content. Tags appear on dashboard cards and act as filters — click a tag to see only captures with that category. Create tags like "backend", "study", "ideas", "work" or any category that makes sense for you.

Queues are separate collections for organizing captures by context or priority. The Quick Queue is for fast captures, without categorization. The Default is the general queue. Queues Q1–Q4 (PRO plan) are customizable by theme, project or client. Each queue has a different border color on the cards.

In the NODUS dashboard, use the search bar at the top. Search works in real time and covers the title, question and response of all captures. Combine search with platform or tag filters to find exactly what you're looking for.

In the dashboard, click the platform badges below the search bar. Click ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any other platform to see only captures from that source. Click again to remove the filter.

Projects (PRO plan) let you create completely separate spaces — for example, "Work", "Studies" and "Personal". Each project has its own queues and captures. Switch between projects with a single click without mixing different content.

Yes. Open the card in the dashboard and click Edit. You can modify the title, add or remove tags and move the capture between queues. The original content (question and response) is preserved.

Use the text search in the dashboard — it covers the full content of all captures, not just the title. If you remember any keyword from the response, just type it. Combine with the platform filter (e.g. Claude) if you know where it came from.

Chains

6 questions

Chains let you connect captures from different conversations into a logical sequence. Imagine you researched a topic on ChatGPT, then went deeper on Claude and found examples on Gemini — a Chain links those three captures into a continuous thread of reasoning, even coming from different platforms.

In the Chains tab of the dashboard, click "New Chain". Give the chain a name and add existing captures by dragging them into the desired order. You can add captures from any platform and rearrange them at any time.

Yes — that is one of the main advantages of Chains. You can connect captures from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and any other supported platform in a single sequence. Each card retains the identification of its source platform.

Open the Chain in the dashboard and click Export. You can export the complete sequence as DOCX or HTML — the result is a structured document with all questions and responses in order, ideal for reports, studies or documentation.

On the free plan, each Chain can have up to 5 captures. On the PRO plan, the limit is significantly higher, allowing extensive chains for complex projects.

With the injection feature, you can insert the content of a Chain directly into the prompt field of any supported platform. It is like giving the AI the full context of previous research — without manually copying and pasting each response.

Privacy & Security

7 questions

No. NODUS has no server of its own that receives your captures. Everything is stored in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local). No capture, tag or content ever leaves your device.

No. NODUS only reads page content when you actively click Save or Quick. There is no automatic capture, passive monitoring or reading of conversations you have not chosen to save.

Captures are stored in the browser profile of your operating system user. If your user profile on the computer is protected by a password, captures are equally protected. NODUS does not add extra encryption — use your operating system's protections for physical security.

Yes. The NODUS core is available on GitHub under the MIT license. Anyone can audit the code and verify there is no hidden data collection. You do not have to take our word for it — see the code.

When you uninstall the extension, the associated local storage is deleted by the browser. Back up first: go to Settings → Export data to download all your captures as JSON. You can reimport them after reinstalling.

NODUS requests only the permissions needed to work:

  • Read AI pages — to detect responses and inject the buttons
  • Local storage — to save your captures on the device
  • Tab access — to know which platform you are on

No permission for camera, microphone, browsing history or passwords is requested.

The NODUS dashboard works completely offline — you can access, search and export captures without an internet connection. Capturing new conversations requires a connection because the AI platforms themselves need to be accessible.
